# Flags and reply patterns

## Flags (the owner reads these before anything is posted)

| Flag | Triggers |
|---|---|
| `SAFETY-health` | rash, burn, itch, hives, allergic, reaction, breakout, headache, dizzy, nausea, sick, doctor, hospital, injury, cut, choking, harm to a child or pet |
| `SAFETY-contamination` | bugs or ants, mould, hair in product, rotten, opened, unsealed, used, tampered, expired |
| `LEGAL-threat` | lawyer, attorney, sue, small claims, FTC, BBB, attorney general, trading standards, "report you", false advertising |
| `MONEY-dispute` | chargeback, dispute, "went to my bank", credit-card or PayPal claim |
| `ACCUSATION` | scam, fraud, fake, "intentional", "on purpose", retaliation |
| `PERSONAL-data` | phone number, email, street address, order number or a staff member's full name in the review |
| `ABUSE` | slurs, threats, harassment |

Strong words alone are **not** flags: "worst", "never again", "overpriced", "disappointed", or a plain delivery delay.

**Holding reply** (35–60 words): name what they reported, neutrally ("a package that arrived opened"). Say you take it seriously and want to look into it, in different words for each flagged review in the batch (for example "we want to get to the bottom of this", "this needs a proper look from our team", "that's not something we'd brush aside"). Then: "Please email [support email] with your order number so a member of our team can follow up directly." For SAFETY-health, add: "If you're still having symptoms, please stop using it and check with a medical professional." Leave that line out for a short-lived preference issue that has already been resolved.
Never include: cause or fault, "known issue", any remedy (not even the placeholder), arguing, or "tell us more here".

**Internal note:** severity (low/med/high), what to check (order, photos, lot or batch, fulfilment location, similar reviews, the platform's report option), and who should see it (owner, QA, legal or insurance if high).

## Patterns by type (structures, not scripts; vary the wording every time)
- **praise:** specific thanks → echo their detail → optional warm close.
- **mixed:** thanks for the positive → acknowledge the downside without arguing → optional private invite.
- **product-quality** (broke, tarnished, leaked, came unravelled): name the exact problem → sorry for the experience → private channel + remedy. If the reviewer says the formula changed, don't confirm or deny it. Ask what they noticed.
- **not-received / late:** use their number of days or weeks → "we'll check the tracking and [REMEDY]". If they say support ignored them, say "we're sorry your emails went unanswered" and give `[direct contact: name/email of a person]`.
- **damaged / wrong item:** name the damage → ask for photos *privately* → remedy.
- **preference:** taste or fit is personal → mention what they liked → `[exchange/return option]`. If they already exchanged and were happy, just thank them.
- **price:** acknowledge it → thank them for trying it → don't justify it with claims.
- **subscription:** acknowledge the specific change they noticed → thank them as a subscriber → private channel → don't promise changes.
- **stock-out:** acknowledge that they keep coming back → `[restock date]` → no invented dates.
- **service-complaint:** apologise for how the interaction felt → direct contact → don't defend the staff.
