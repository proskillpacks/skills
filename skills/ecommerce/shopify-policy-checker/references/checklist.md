# Policy checklist

Each item: what to look for, and why a customer or platform cares. "Must" = flag as missing if absent. "Should" = flag as Medium/Low.

## Refund / return policy (25 points)

| # | Item | Level |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Return window in days, and **from when** (delivery date vs order date) | Must |
| R2 | Condition of returned items (unused, tags on, original packaging) | Must |
| R3 | How to start a return (portal link, email, account page), plus whether a return authorization is needed | Must |
| R4 | Who pays return shipping; whether a prepaid label is provided; label cost if deducted | Must |
| R5 | Refund method (original payment, store credit, exchange) and **timing** (e.g. within X business days of receiving the return) | Must |
| R6 | Items that can't be returned (final sale, gift cards, personalised, perishable, hygiene, digital), stated clearly | Must if any exist |
| R7 | Damaged, defective or wrong item: how to report, within how many days, photo requirement, who pays | Must |
| R8 | Exchanges: allowed or not, and how | Should |
| R9 | Restocking fee: amount, or say there isn't one | Should |
| R10 | Sale and discounted items: same rules or different | Should |
| R11 | Late or missing refunds: what to do | Should |
| R12 | Store credit: does it expire? | Should if store credit is offered |
| R13 | Contact for returns (email) | Must |

## Shipping policy (25 points)

| # | Item | Level |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Processing time before dispatch (business days), and cut-off time if any | Must |
| S2 | Shipping methods with estimated delivery times per region | Must |
| S3 | Costs: flat, calculated, or free-shipping threshold (the same number everywhere on the site) | Must |
| S4 | Where you ship (countries/regions) and where you don't (PO boxes, APO/FPO, islands) | Must |
| S5 | International: who pays duties and taxes (prices include them, or the customer pays on delivery) | Must if shipping internationally |
| S6 | Tracking: when and how the customer gets it | Should |
| S7 | Lost, stolen or "delivered but not received" packages: what you do, deadline to report | Should |
| S8 | Wrong address entered by the customer: what happens | Should |
| S9 | Delays and peak season / pre-orders / back-orders: what to expect | Should |
| S10 | Local pickup / local delivery if offered | Should |

## Privacy policy (20 points)

| # | Item | Level |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Store's legal name and contact (email, and postal address for EU/UK) | Must |
| P2 | What personal data is collected (order, payment, device, cookies) | Must |
| P3 | Why it's used | Must |
| P4 | Who it's shared with (Shopify, payment processors, shipping carriers, analytics, ad platforms) | Must |
| P5 | Cookies and tracking, with a way to opt out | Must |
| P6 | Customer rights (access, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing) and how to use them | Must |
| P7 | Last-updated date | Should (CalOPPA expects an effective date) |
| P8 | Data retention | Should |
| P9 | Children's data | Should |
| P10 | Matches reality: names apps and services the store actually uses; no leftover template placeholders | Must |

## Terms of service + contact information (15 points)

| # | Item | Level |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Business name and contact method on the contact-information policy or a contact page | Must |
| T2 | Terms don't contradict the refund or shipping policy (common: boilerplate "all sales final" vs a 30-day return policy) | Must |
| T3 | Governing law matches where the business is | Should |
| T4 | Price errors, order cancellation by the store | Should |
| T5 | Subscription terms (if subscriptions are sold): billing frequency, how to cancel, renewal reminders | Must if subscriptions exist |

## Consistency (15 points)

Compare across every source in the bundle: policies, FAQ/pages, homepage banners and product pages.
- Return window, fees, final-sale rules
- Free-shipping threshold and currency
- Processing and delivery times
- Warranty length and coverage
- Contact email, phone, address, and business name (watch for old brand names after a rebrand)
- Countries served

## Region rules (apply only to regions the store plausibly sells to)

- **EU / EEA:** consumers get a 14-day right of withdrawal for most online purchases (Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU), counted from delivery. Sellers must explain how to withdraw. The refund is due within 14 days of the withdrawal, but the seller may wait until the goods are back. If the seller doesn't tell the customer they must pay return costs, the seller pays. Trader identity and a geographic address must be given. "No returns" or "store credit only" for EU consumers is a High issue.
- **UK:** similar 14-day cancellation right (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013), and goods must match their description and be of satisfactory quality (Consumer Rights Act 2015). The UK has no fixed "guarantee period": 6 years (5 in Scotland) is the time limit for bringing a claim, so don't call it a 6-year guarantee. Within 30 days of delivery, faulty goods can be rejected for a full refund.
- **Australia:** consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law apply whatever the policy says. "No refunds" signs are misleading, and policies shouldn't suggest otherwise for faulty items.
- **US:** the FTC Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule: ship within the time you state, or within 30 days if you don't state one. If there's a delay, tell the customer and offer them the choice to cancel. California: a privacy policy with an effective date (CalOPPA); CCPA/CPRA rights for businesses that meet its thresholds.
- **Canada:** privacy (PIPEDA) expects a contact for privacy questions and a description of purposes.

## Platform reasons (why owners care)

- **Shopify checkout** links the policies in Settings > Policies. An empty policy there is a dead link at checkout.
- **Shopify return rules** (Settings > Policies > Return rules) should match the text: same window, fees and final-sale items.
- **Google Merchant Center** asks merchants to provide a return policy and shipping information that match what's on the website. Mismatches and missing contact or return information are common reasons for "misrepresentation" reviews. Keep the numbers identical everywhere.
- **Chargebacks:** in a dispute, the refund policy the customer could see at checkout is part of the evidence, so a clear and consistent policy helps.
