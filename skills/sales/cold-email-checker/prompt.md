# Cold email checker: paste-in prompt

Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any chatbot. Paste your draft and the text of the prospect's page it refers to.

---

You review a cold email draft before it is sent. Test claims against evidence. Do not write a new campaign. Never add a fact that is not in my draft, the page text below or my notes.

Draft (subject and body):
[paste]

Text from the prospect's page(s) the draft refers to (if I have none, write "none" and mark every claim about them "not checked against the site"):
[paste]

What I can prove about myself (results, clients, years): [list, or "nothing"]
Sender country and recipient country (optional): [ ]

Do this:
1. List every factual claim about the prospect. For each, quote the evidence from the page text, or mark it Unsupported or Not checked.
2. List every claim about me and mark it Backed (I said it is true) or Needs proof.
3. Flag phrases that imply I used the product, read their newsletter or met them, unless I said so.
4. Check the subject line (under about 6 words, accurately reflects the body, no "Re:" or "Fwd:" on a first message, no fake urgency).
5. Count the asks (one is right) and the words (flag over 120 for a first message).
6. Commercial email basics, per the US FTC's CAN-SPAM guide: accurate From, To and Reply-To details, a subject line that matches the content, a clear disclosure that it is an ad, my valid physical postal address, a clear opt-out. Opt-outs must be honoured within 10 business days, and the law makes no exception for business-to-business email. Say which are present or missing. Say that other countries' rules differ and I must check the recipient's. This is not legal advice.

Output: Verdict (Send as is, Send after fixes, or Do not send, with the main reason), a claims table, numbered issues quoting my exact words, a tightened version with unsupported claims removed or marked [CONFIRM: ...] and one ask, and a "Before you send" list. Do not guess the recipient's name. Plain short sentences, no em dashes.
