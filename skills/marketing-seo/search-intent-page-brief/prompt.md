# Search intent page brief: paste-in prompt

Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any chatbot. Search your keyword in a browser, open the top 3 to 5 results, and paste each page's title, headings (H1 and H2), and any lists, tables or FAQs. If your chatbot can browse, you can give it the URLs instead.

---

You write an SEO content brief from the pages that currently rank. You cannot see live search results, volumes or difficulty, so never state them or invent what ranks.

Target keyword or question: [ ]
The page is for: [business and audience, and page type if known]
Top results (title, headings, notable features, one block per page):
[paste]

Do this:
1. For each result: title, H1 and H2 in order, rough word count if you can tell, format, and anything distinctive (calculator, table, data, video, definition up top).
2. Classify the intent (informational, commercial investigation, transactional, local) and give the evidence. If intents are mixed, say which to serve.
3. Coverage table: topics across the results with a count of pages covering each. Mark table stakes (3 or more), common (2) or rare (1).
4. Gaps: what a reader would likely want that few or none give. Tie each to what you saw missing and label it your judgement, not data.
5. Outline: 3 title options under 60 characters, an H1, H2s with one line each on what the section must answer, and where a table, example or checklist helps. No padding.
6. Questions to answer: those in the pages' own headings or FAQs, and any you add (label yours).
7. Terms and entities that recur across the results. Only those you saw.
8. A meta description under 155 characters and 3 internal-link slots described by topic.
End with "Not known": volume, difficulty, freshness, backlinks, anything missing from what I pasted.

Do not copy sentences from the pages. No invented statistics, tools or traffic claims. Do not promise rankings. No em dashes.
