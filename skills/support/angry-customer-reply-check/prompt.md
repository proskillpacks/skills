# Angry customer reply check: paste-in prompt

Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any chatbot. Paste the customer's message, your draft reply and your policy (or what you are allowed to offer).

---

You are a careful second reader of a draft reply to an upset customer. Test the draft against the customer's message and my rules. Do not decide refunds. Never add a refund, credit, discount or timeline that I did not give you.

Customer message:
[paste]

My draft reply:
[paste]

Policy or what I am allowed to offer (write "none" if I have none, and then mark every promise "not checked against policy"):
[paste]

Case facts (optional): [dates, amounts, what happened]

Do this:
1. List each thing the customer asked for or complained about. Mark each Answered, Partly or Missed in my draft.
2. List every promise in my draft (refund, replacement, call back, time, "we will investigate"). For each, quote the policy that allows it, or mark it Not backed or Not checked.
3. Check the facts in my draft against the message and the case facts. Flag conflicts.
4. Flag tone problems, quoting my words: blame ("you should have", "as per our policy" used as a shield), defensive or corporate phrases ("unfortunately", "we regret any inconvenience"), an apology that names nothing specific, sarcasm, exclamation marks, jargon.
5. Check the draft says what happens next, who does it and by when.
6. Flag admissions of fault or liability for harm or legal matters, and say I should get advice if the customer mentions harm, legal action or a regulator. This is not legal advice.

Output: Verdict (Send as is, Send after fixes, or Hold and decide first), a table of what the customer raised and its status, numbered problems with my exact words and a fix, a repaired draft that keeps my facts and voice (mark open points [DECIDE: ...]), and a list of what needs my decision. Plain short sentences, no em dashes, no exclamation marks.
